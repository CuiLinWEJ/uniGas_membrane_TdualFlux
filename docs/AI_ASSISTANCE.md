# AI Assistance Statement

## Purpose

This document discloses the use of generative AI during the post-thesis software-development process and distinguishes AI assistance from the maintainer's technical work.

## Scope of AI Assistance

ChatGPT was used interactively for activities including:

- technical discussion;
- debugging suggestions;
- review of possible implementation issues;
- alternative code approaches;
- explanation of programming concepts; and
- documentation drafting/editing.

The workflow was iterative: the maintainer identified a problem, supplied relevant code or run output, evaluated suggested changes, modified/integrated the code, executed the solver, inspected the resulting behaviour, and returned additional errors/results for further debugging when needed.

## Human Contributions and Decisions

The repository maintainer performed the human technical work required to turn suggestions into the maintained implementation, including:

- identification of the implementation issue;
- definition of the development objective;
- selection or rejection of proposed approaches;
- source-code integration and modification;
- compilation and execution;
- preparation of numerical cases;
- inspection of solver output and numerical behaviour;
- maintenance of the final source version; and
- organization of the public repository.

AI suggestions were not treated as authoritative and do not constitute validation.

## Validation Responsibility

The current repository is still a research prototype. Although the maintainer has performed preliminary software/numerical checks, comprehensive physical validation remains incomplete. Users should independently verify the implementation for their own scientific or engineering use.

## Authorship Statement

The maintainer claims authorship only for original human-authored contributions, modifications, selection/arrangement, and documentation to the extent protected by applicable law. No claim is made to upstream OpenFOAM/uniGasFoam material, and no exclusive copyright claim is made over material that lacks sufficient human creative contribution.

ChatGPT/OpenAI is not listed as an author, maintainer, or endorser of this repository.
